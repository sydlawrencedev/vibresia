### 4.2 Parked for next rotation

#### Two SERP titles are over the 60 character limit

| Page | Current title | Length | Target query | GSC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| /comparison/vibresia-vs-synesthesia | `Synesthesia Alternative for Mac (2026): Vibresia vs Synesthesia` | 63 | synesthesia free alternative | 2 impr, 0 clicks, pos 63.50 |
| /comparison/vibresia-vs-shadertoy | `Shadertoy Alternative for Music (2026): Vibresia vs Shadertoy` | 61 | shadertoy alternative | 0 impr |

The project's stated limit is 60 characters for a title and 158 for a description. The code is slightly looser than the policy: `generate-seo-pages.mjs` throws over 160 for a description, and the test allows a title of 65.

**Why parked.** Both pages are in the comparison cluster and appear in `scripts/locked-routes.sh`. The generator and shared data changed in the window, so every page that consumes them is locked. The brief is at `agents/content-brand/work/2026-11-12-seo-audit-titles.md`. The next run can apply two lines once the lock expires.

**Worth being honest about the size of this.** The Synesthesia page has 2 impressions at position 63.50 and the Shadertoy page has none. A title fix will not move either page much on its own. It is worth doing because the titles are over the limit and the fix is nearly free, not because the data says it will help.

#### `/blog/apple-music-visualizer` needs a content-strategy answer, not a title answer

This is the only Group B page. It has the site's largest page impression count, 16, and the widest position spread, from 5.0 to 99.0. Google is testing it against several queries and is not satisfied.

The page is 1,319 words with a direct answer at the top, an FAQ block and full schema. It is not thin. The spread suggests the page is being considered for "apple music visualizer" at position 48.62 and not placed.

**Do not write a title brief for this yet.** Two impressions at position 99 for "music visualizer iphone" and one at 46 for "mac music visualizer" are two different searches with two different intents, and the page cannot serve both. This needs a decision about which query the page is for, which is a content-strategy call rather than an SEO audit call. Parked until the lock expires on 2026-11-13 and more data has accumulated.

#### The lock is wider than it looks, and it may be too wide

`scripts/locked-routes.sh` reports 82 locked routes. Every page with GSC impressions is locked. The reason is that three commits in the window regenerated the whole site:

| Commit | Date | Pages regenerated |
|---|---|---|
| 9560fdb | 2026-11-05 | 52 |
| c2735d8 | 2026-11-06 | 54 |
| b9dd33c | 2026-11-06 | 51 |

For the two title pages, the actual diff in the window is small: a button colour for contrast, a footer legal-links row, and the author's description inside the schema. None of it touches the page's own title, H1, description or body copy.

**This is a question for the harvest routine, not a change to make here.** Two options, both for the owner:

1. Narrow the shared-file lock to the shared files that carry page content (`content/data/`, `content/pages/`), and let the generator and the chrome partials lock only the pages they change in a way that touches copy.
2. Keep the rule as it is, and accept that a whole-site regeneration locks the site for seven days.

The second is what happened this week, and the routine's instruction to pick the next unlocked opportunity had no next opportunity.
