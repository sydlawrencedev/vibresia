market scanner checks this list for new entrants and the harvest uses it to
decide which comparisons are worth writing.

in `site/comparison/`.

## New competitor bar

A new product joins this list when:

1. It launches and is usable on macOS, and
2. It does one of: live music-reactive rendering on Mac, shader-based visuals
   an AI can write, or a free Mac visualiser that plays out of the box.

A "5 alternatives to X" post is written **only** once search data shows 20 or
more impressions a month for the brand X. Record the check either way, in
`docs/market-scan/`.

## What Vibresia is compared on

The comparison pages argue five things, in this order:

1. **Free.** No account, no ads, no trial, no price.
2. **Plays out of the box.** No build step, no plugin, no project file.
3. **Mac-native.** Apple silicon, macOS 14.2 or later.
4. **Shader-based.** A vibescape is one function, so an AI assistant can write
   one and preview it.
5. **Privacy.** Audio is analysed on the Mac and never leaves it.

Facts about Vibresia come from `docs/product-facts.md`. Facts about a
competitor come from that competitor's own comparison page, checked in the same
run.
