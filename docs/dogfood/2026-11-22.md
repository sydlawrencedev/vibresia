# Dogfood testing: Vibresia, 2026-11-22

**Target: production, `https://vibresia.com`.** The routine's default
`http://localhost:3000` has nothing listening on it. This repository is a
static site in `site/`, a Cloudflare Worker in `worker/index.js`, and a macOS
app. There is no Next.js dev server. The production site is where a visitor
actually is, and it is the only target that exercises the Worker, Cloudflare's
edge, and the real routes.

**Verdict: no new defects on the site. Zero console errors across every page
and every flow, zero broken links, zero broken images, no horizontal overflow
at any width, and the four issues fixed on 12 November all held. Two things
were found, and both are metadata defects that belong to the SEO audit
routine: five meta descriptions and three page titles are over the project's
own length limits. Those are shipped in the SEO audit's PR. One accessibility
finding is carried over and still deliberately left alone, for the reason
given on 12 November.**

## 3. Issues found

### The SERP metadata is over the project's own length limits

The audit sweep found eight pages whose metadata is longer than the limits the
project sets for itself. The code is the authority: `scripts/generate-seo-pages.mjs`
throws when a generated page's description goes over 160 characters, and the
previous audit recorded that the code's title allowance (65) is looser than
the policy (60).

**Descriptions over 160 characters:**

| Page | Length | Where the text lives |
|---|---|---|
| `/agents` | 251 | `site/agents.html` |
| `/stats/data/what-mac-music-visualiser-visitors-look-for` | 209 | `site/stats/data/what-mac-music-visualiser-visitors-look-for.html` |
| `/` | 190 | `site/index.html` |
| `/author/syd-lawrence` | 168 | `site/author/syd-lawrence.html` |
| `/share/band` | 161 | `site/share/band.html` |

**Titles over 60 characters:**

| Page | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| `/stats/data/what-mac-music-visualiser-visitors-look-for` | 75 | `What people look for when they look for a Mac music visualiser — Vibresia` |
| `/comparison/vibresia-vs-synesthesia` | 63 | `Synesthesia Alternative for Mac (2026): Vibresia vs Synesthesia` |
| `/comparison/vibresia-vs-shadertoy` | 61 | `Shadertoy Alternative for Music (2026): Vibresia vs Shadertoy` |

**Why these are the SEO audit's and not a dogfood fix.** The two long
comparison titles were already found by the SEO audit on 12 November and
parked because the pages were locked. They are the same two pages. The
description findings are new, and they are all on pages the generator does not
guard, which is why the build has never caught them: `/`, `/agents` and
`/author/syd-lawrence` are hand-written HTML, and the share pages and the
statistics page are generated by other scripts that carry no length check at
all.

Both sets are fixed and shipped in the SEO audit's pull request. This report
records them so the dogfood log shows the site was measured against the same
standard the audit uses.

**How they were measured.** Length was taken from the rendered page on
production, not from the source, so what is counted is what a crawler reads.
The three titles over the limit were counted from the `<title>` element. The
two comparison titles are generated from `content/pages/`, so their fix is a
change to the page source plus a regeneration.

## 4. Left alone, on purpose

### The gallery card alt text repeats the visible title

**Severity: low, and carried over unchanged from 12 November.** On
`/vibescapes`, each card's image has `alt="Midnight Stars Bass Twinkle v2"`
and the card's visible title is the same string. axe reports
`image-redundant-alt`, which is a best-practice rule and not a WCAG one.

It is still not fixed, for the reason given on 12 November: the image is the
card's main visual, and the alt is its accessible name in every context where
the text is not rendered. Making it `alt=""` would be right for a decorative
image beside visible text and wrong for an image whose label might be hidden
by a layout that does not exist yet. One rule on one page, and no user is
blocked by it.

Measured today: 6 nodes, 1 page out of 79. Unchanged.

### The gallery images read as broken on first scroll

Twenty-nine of the thirty-one images on `/vibescapes` report
`naturalWidth: 1200` and `complete: true` after the sweep forces them eager.
None returned a 4xx, and none is missing an `alt` attribute. The apparent
breakage before scroll is lazy loading doing its job. This was in the 12
November report and is still true. It is not a defect.

## 5. Things that looked like bugs and were not

### `/install` returns 404

The no-JavaScript check flagged `/install` as a 404 with no H1 and no links.
`/install` is not a route. It appears in no sitemap, and the site serves
`/install.md` (200) and `/install.sh` (200, the script the install command
runs). The check guessed a URL. Not a defect.

### 51 pages appeared to be missing a `lang` attribute

The structure sweep reported no `lang` on 51 of 78 pages. Checked against
production, every page has one: `/` and `/privacy` carry `lang="en"`, while
the generated pages carry `lang="en-GB"`. The sweep read the attribute
wrong. Not a defect.

There is a real inconsistency behind it, though it is not one the audit
raises as a defect: the hand-written pages say `en` and the generated pages
say `en-GB`. The house style is UK English, so `en-GB` is the better value,
and it is noted here rather than fixed, because changing it is a
site-wide consistency decision rather than a bug fix.

### Chrome's accessibility tree hides the `/comparison` heading skip

Carried over from 12 November. The heading skip is gone from the DOM and the
accessibility tree still does not show it. Not a defect in the site's
markup.
