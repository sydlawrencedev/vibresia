## 2. New competitors

The bar in `docs/competitors.md`: a new product joins the set when it launches and is usable
on macOS, and does one of live music-reactive rendering on Mac, shader-based visuals an AI can
write, or a free Mac visualiser that plays out of the box.

### 2.1 Met the bar: Prism (Sawyer Christensen)

- **What it is.** A native macOS MilkDrop and projectM visualiser. It captures system audio
  through Core Audio Process Taps and renders `.milk` preset files through the real vendored
  libprojectM 4.x engine, bridged into Metal through an EGL/ANGLE context.
- **Why it matters.** It is the first Mac-native app to put the genuine projectM engine behind
  a finished App Store product, which is the exact ground the projectM comparison page says
  projectM does not cover.
- **Platform and price.** Mac only. Requires macOS 26.0 or later and an M1 chip or later.
  Free on the Mac App Store. 25.1 MB. Copyright 2026.
- **Sources.** [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/prism-music-visualizer/id6798428433),
  [source repository](https://github.com/SawyerChristensen/Prism) (2 stars, 121 commits, 1 open
  issue, "Deeper audio-reactive control for live music performance", opened 3 October 2026).
- **New competitor bar check.** Launched, usable on macOS, and it does live music-reactive
  rendering on Mac. **Met. Add to `docs/competitors.md`.**
- **Search demand check.** Live Search Console export, 28 days to 9 November 2026
  (`ops/seo/pulse/gsc-export.py`, property `sc-domain:vibresia.com`): **no query containing
  "prism" appears at all.** The site's highest-impression query has 7 impressions. The bar is
  20 or more impressions a month for the brand. **Not met. No "5 alternatives to Prism" post.**
- **Recommendation to GTM.** Add to the competitor set and build a comparison page record.
  The page to write is `comparison/vibresia-vs-prism`, and it has a real argument: Prism is
  free and plays `.milk` presets, which Vibresia does not. Say that plainly. Vibresia's case
  is 18 built-in scenes that need no preset pack, writing your own scene as one GLSL function
  or a sentence to an AI assistant, and the ability to share a scene as a link that plays in a
  browser. Note that Prism needs macOS 26.0 and an M1 or later, where Vibresia needs macOS 14.2.

### 2.2 Met the bar: SYQEL (Desktop, Live and Art)

- **What it is.** A suite of three apps from one maker: SYQEL Desktop for DJs, VJs and venues,
  SYQEL Live for streaming on camera, and SYQEL Art for listening. Desktop runs on Windows,
  macOS and in the browser. Art runs in a browser and on iOS, iPad, Android, macOS, Windows,
  Android TV, Fire TV and Apple TV.
- **Why it matters.** It is the most direct new threat to the listening end of Vibresia's
  audience. SYQEL Art does what Vibresia does on a Mac, hears the Mac's system audio with no
  virtual device, and is free to try.
- **Platform and price.** Desktop needs macOS 12 or later. Desktop is free to try with a SYQEL
  watermark on the output, then US$35 a month or US$299 a year. Art is free with a watermark
  and Volume 1 only, then US$10 a month or US$79 a year. Live is US$25 a month or US$249 a
  year. A Gig Pass is US$20 for 24 hours.
- **Sources.** [Pricing](https://www.syqel.com/pricing), [products](https://www.syqel.com/products),
  [SYQEL Art on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/syqel-art-music-visualizer/id6783419598),
  [SYQEL Desktop support](https://www.syqel.com/support/creating-a-virtual-audio-input-mac).
- **New competitor bar check.** Launched, usable on macOS, and it does live music-reactive
  rendering on Mac. **Met. Add to `docs/competitors.md`.**
- **Search demand check.** Same live GSC export: **no query containing "syqel" appears at all.**
  **Not met. No "5 alternatives to SYQEL" post.**
- **Recommendation to GTM.** Add to the competitor set. SYQEL is also the single most useful
  source of competitor complaint themes this run, because their own documentation, pricing
  pages and terms spell out the catches in their free tier. Use those, not their marketing.

### 2.3 Met the bar: RENDERWAVE (Wesley Walz)

- **What it is.** A Mac-native VJ instrument built on Swift and Metal 3 for Apple silicon. It
  ships 85 shaders, 47 performance presets, per-parameter audio modulation across bass, mid,
  mid-high and high, MIDI, Ableton Link and Syphon output, and 8K output through MetalFX.
- **Why it matters.** It is a Mac-native live performer tool in the same territory as
  Synesthesia and TouchDesigner, and it publishes a comparison blog of its own.
- **Platform and price.** macOS on Apple silicon. US$9.99 a month, US$99 a year, or US$299
  perpetual. A 14-day full-access trial with a card. A free edition capped at 720p on one Mac
  with no watermark and no time limit.
- **Sources.** [renderwave.io](https://renderwave.io/), [features](https://renderwave.io/features),
  [about](https://renderwave.io/about).
- **New competitor bar check.** Launched and usable on macOS, and it does live music-reactive
  rendering on Mac. **Met. Add to `docs/competitors.md`.**
- **Search demand check.** Same live GSC export: **no query containing "renderwave" or
  "render wave" appears at all.** **Not met. No "5 alternatives to RENDERWAVE" post.**
- **Recommendation to GTM.** Add to the competitor set, in the same category as Synesthesia and
  TouchDesigner. Vibresia is not a substitute for it and the comparison page must not pretend
  otherwise. The honest line is that RENDERWAVE wins for a live set and Vibresia is free and
  ready to watch at home.

### 2.4 Met the bar: Animus Visualiser (blankensmith, itch.io)

- **What it is.** A free 3D music visualiser for macOS that reacts in real time, with keyboard
  controls for live performance and Syphon output. Microphone and line-in input.
- **Why it matters.** It is free, Mac-native and has Syphon output, which is the combination
  Vibresia does not have. It is also the oldest free Mac visualiser still in circulation, and
  its own itch.io page shows the complaint themes clearly.
- **Platform and price.** macOS. Free, name your own price on itch.io. Two builds listed:
  Animus.zip (6.6 MB) and Animus1.0.1.zip (76 MB). Rated 5.0 out of 5 from 1 rating.
- **Sources.** [itch.io listing](https://blankensmith.itch.io/animus-visualizer),
  [the project site](https://www.torinblankensmith.com/projects/animus-visualizer),
  [a modernised fork](https://james-see.github.io/animus-visualizer) by James Campbell.
- **New competitor bar check.** Launched, usable on macOS, and it is a free Mac visualiser
  that plays out of the box. **Met. Add to `docs/competitors.md`.**
- **Search demand check.** Same live GSC export: **no query containing "animus" appears at
  all.** **Not met. No "5 alternatives to Animus" post.**
- **Recommendation to GTM.** Add to the competitor set. It has a small audience and a real
  weakness that Vibresia can name: it takes a microphone or line-in, so to react to music
  playing on the Mac a user has to install a virtual audio device and set up a multi-output
  device. Vibresia listens to the system audio directly.

## 3. Competitor review complaints, counted by theme

### 3.1 What was reachable and what was not

**Trustpilot was not readable.** Every attempt returned HTTP 403: the raw fetch of
`trustpilot.com/review/<domain>` for all fourteen competitor domains, and the web fetch tool on
`https://www.trustpilot.com/review/soundspectrum.com`. The response body was a 991-byte
bot-challenge page. **No Trustpilot review was read, and no Trustpilot count in this scan is
real.** Trustpilot is also, on this evidence, a poor fit for this competitor set: the direct
Mac competitors are desktop software sold on the Mac App Store or by direct licence, and the
one Trustpilot profile for a visualiser product found (Videobolt) is a browser template maker
with no relationship to anything we compare against.

**The App Store was only partly readable.** Three approaches were tried:

1. Direct fetch of `apps.apple.com/us/app/<id>` returned **HTTP 429** (rate limited) for every
   app tried, including ones with plenty of reviews.
2. The App Store review feed at `itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=<id>/sortby=mostrecent/json`
   returned HTTP 200 for every app tried but **zero review entries in every case**, including
   apps known to have written reviews (Vibely, 56 ratings and visible written reviews on the
   App Store web page). The feed is not serving review data to this environment.
3. **Web search worked.** Search surfaced the App Store listing pages, including the written
   review text and dates. Every App Store count below comes from method 3, and every one is
   dated so it can be checked.

**Google Play was readable** for the Android-only products, through search.

**GitHub and itch.io were readable directly.** projectM's own release notes and issue tracker,
and the Animus itch.io community page, gave the clearest complaint themes in the scan.

The consequence, stated plainly: **the counts below are small.** They are real, dated and
linked, but this run did not read the full 1 and 2 star review set for every competitor. The
themes with a count of 3 or more are the ones that came up repeatedly across independent
sources, not the result of an exhaustive read.
