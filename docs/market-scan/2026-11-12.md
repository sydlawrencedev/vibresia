## 2. New competitors

The bar in `docs/competitors.md`: a new product joins the set when it launches and is usable
on macOS, and does one of live music-reactive rendering on Mac, shader-based visuals an AI
can write, or a free Mac visualiser that plays out of the box.

### 2.1 Met the bar: LIQUIDWAVE (Appnics LLC)

- **What it is.** A Mac music visualiser with 70+ scenes in ten styles (Line, Graphic, Wire,
  Raster, Lighting, Chroma, Cyber, Metal, Fluid, Spectral). It listens to system audio
  through macOS's own system-audio capture, and it also has a Listen Mode that takes the
  room through the microphone. Analysis is on-device: seven frequency bands, sixty times a
  second, beat and tempo tracking.
- **Platform and price.** macOS 14.2 or later, Apple silicon **and Intel**. Windows and
  Linux downloads too, and iPhone, iPad and Android builds. Signed with Appnics' Developer
  ID and notarised by Apple. The download is free and includes two scenes (Wave, and one the
  user picks). The rest unlock inside the app for **US$34.99 once**. On the App Store it is
  **US$39.99 once, or US$19.99 a year**.
- **Launch.** First public release of the Mac download was **13 September 2026**, version
  1.0.0. That is a month before this window, so it is not a launch inside the window. The Mac
  release notes list versions 1.0.1 through 1.6.0 since, and **six of them fall inside this
  window**: 1.2.0 on 14 October, 1.3.0 on 18 October, 1.4.1 on 23 October, 1.5.0 on 26
  October, 1.5.1 on 27 October and 1.6.0 on 29 October. The App Store build is on 1.7.1, and
  1.7.1 landed on 11 November 2026. The release notes list no 1.4.0 heading; the sequence
  runs 1.3.0 (18 Oct) then 1.4.1 (23 Oct).
- **Why it matters.** This is Vibresia's shape. Free download, system audio with no virtual
  device, a large scene library, notarised, privacy stated the same way Vibresia states it
  ("Everything is analyzed on your Mac or PC and the audio is never uploaded"). It also
  ships on Windows and Linux, which Vibresia does not, and it runs on Intel Macs, which
  Vibresia does not.
- **Sources.** [Site](https://liquidwave.app/), [release notes](https://liquidwave.app/whats-new),
  [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/music-visualizer-liquidwave/id6807689549),
  [Spotify guide](https://liquidwave.app/spotify-visualizer-mac).
- **New competitor bar check.** Launched 13 September 2026 in Global, usable on macOS, and
  it is a free Mac visualiser that plays out of the box. **Met. Add to `docs/competitors.md`.**
- **Search demand check.** Live Search Console export, 28 days to 10 November 2026
  (`ops/seo/pulse/data/gsc-28d-latest.json`, property `sc-domain:vibresia.com`): **no query
  containing "liquidwave" or "liquid wave" appears at all.** The site's highest-impression
  query has 8 impressions. The bar is 20 or more impressions a month for the brand.
  **Not met. No "5 alternatives to LIQUIDWAVE" post.**
- **Recommendation to GTM.** Add to the competitor set and add a record to
  `content/data/competitors.json` before any page may name it. The record needs the price
  (US$34.99 once on the direct download, US$39.99 once or US$19.99 a year on the App
  Store), the platform, the licence and the date verified. The comparison page to write is
  `comparison/vibresia-vs-liquidwave`. The argument is close and has to be written honestly:
  LIQUIDWAVE has more scenes than Vibresia's 18, ships on Windows and Linux, and runs on
  Intel Macs. Vibresia's case is that its 18 scenes are built in, that a new scene is one
  GLSL function you or an AI assistant writes, and that the scene can be shared as a link
  that plays in a browser.

per-review star count is rendered as SVG.** The product-level rating block does carry the
distribution, so a product's star split is verifiable even where an individual review's
stars are not.

That set also rotates. The plain review URL and the `&platform=mac` variant of the same app
serve different reviews: the Vythm VJ page with `&platform=mac` carries a 23 June 2023
1-star review ("It shows this app was eating over 30gb of ram") that the plain URL does not
show, and the plain URL carries a 13 May 2023 review that the `platform=mac` variant does
not. **Every quote in this file was verified against the live page at the URL given**, and
where a quote comes from one variant of the URL, the link given is that variant.

**Google Play was partly readable.** The Play listing pages for Muviz, Muviz Edge and
Music Visualizer (h6ah4i) were fetched directly. They serve three highlighted reviews each,

**The thesis.** Two free Mac visualisers that both hear system audio with no virtual
device, and they differ on what you can do next. LIQUIDWAVE gives you a curated catalogue.
Vibresia gives you a function you can change.

**The argument.** LIQUIDWAVE's Mac build uses the system-audio capture macOS has shipped
since 14.2, so it hears Spotify, Apple Music, a browser tab or a DAW with no plug-in and no
loopback driver, and it runs on Apple silicon and Intel. Its catalogue is 70+ scenes in ten
styles, and the maker says plainly that they are curated, not generated: "Never a random
variation, never a generic gradient." New styles join the set over time. On the Mac, Mix
Shuffle reads the track's title, artist and album, works out its genre, energy, mood and
texture, and shuffles the scenes that fit it, all on the Mac.

Vibresia ships 18 built-in scenes and a different way to make more. A vibescape is one
GLSL function, `vec3 render(vec2 p, float t)`. The app compiles it, draws it and reports
errors in plain words before it saves. Pointer, keyboard and MIDI keyboard are available as
inputs. An AI assistant can write one for you, locally over the app's MCP server or over
the public server at `https://vibresia.com/mcp`, and it can then compile it, preview it and
save it. A scene you like can be shared as a short link that plays in any browser.

**What Vibresia does not have.** LIQUIDWAVE runs on Windows and Linux, and on Intel Macs.
Vibresia is macOS 14.2 or later, Apple silicon only. LIQUIDWAVE has more scenes than
Vibresia. LIQUIDWAVE ships on iPhone, iPad and Android. Vibresia has no mobile or Windows
version and no web app that visualises your own music.

**The price, side by side.** LIQUIDWAVE's download is free and includes two scenes, with
the rest behind US$34.99 once, or US$39.99 once or US$19.99 a year on the App Store.
Vibresia is free. Every feature, no trial, no expiry, no ads, no account, no subscription.
Open source under the MIT licence. The optional donation changes nothing in the app.
