I make Vibresia, the free Mac app that turns whatever your Mac is playing into visuals, at Minith Labs. I write its code, its source is public, and I write the comparison pages on this site.

How the comparison pages are written

Every fact about another app, its price, what it runs on, what it does well and where it falls short, comes from that app's own website, store listing, documentation or code. Each one is stored with the page it came from and the day it was checked, and the site won't build if a fact is missing its source or is more than a year old.

Where I haven't used an app myself, the page says so and describes what its maker says it does. Prices are shown in the currency the maker uses, and left out when the maker doesn't say which currency it is.

Vibresia is my app, so I have a reason to favour it. Each page says who the other apps are better for, and a list puts the better pick first when Vibresia isn't it.

Something wrong?

If a price has changed or a page gets something wrong, open an issue and I'll fix it.
