The gallery

Every vibescape, in one place.

The ones that come with Vibresia, and the ones people have written and shared, newest first. Open one and it plays in your copy of Vibresia.

How the gallery works
  • Your name goes on it, and it is public. Nothing is listed here without the tick in the share sheet.
  • You can take it out yourself, at any time, from the share sheet.
  • Name it so people can find it, and say who made it. The name is a link to everything else that person has shared.
  • Nothing here is read before it goes in. If something should not be here, tell us and it comes down.

Questions people ask

What is in this gallery?

The vibescapes that come with Vibresia, and the ones people have written and shared, newest first. A vibescape is one GLSL function that draws to the music.

Who can put something in the gallery?

Whoever made it, from the app or from an AI assistant. You tick it into the gallery when you share it, and your name goes on it. You can take it out again at any time.

Is my name public?

Yes, if you tick it into the gallery. The name on the vibescape is shown next to it here, where anybody can find it. It is not shown anywhere else.

Do I need Vibresia to look at one?

No. Each vibescape has a page that draws it live in your browser, so it plays whether or not you have the app. The app adds the Mac app's own audio and the rest of the scenes.

Can I make one of these?

Yes. Point an AI assistant at Vibresia and it writes one, or write the GLSL yourself. Either way it is a single function, and the app draws it before you keep it.
