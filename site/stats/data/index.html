Original statistics

Figures from our own traffic.

Short version: these are our own numbers, published with the method behind them, so anybody can check them.

What is here

Every other visualiser roundup quotes somebody else's figures. These are ours: counts from this site's own anonymous traffic, about a question our visitors actually asked.

Each one comes with the method behind it — who was counted, over which dates, how the count was made, how many people it covers and what it does not mean — and with the numbers in machine-readable form, so you do not have to take our word for any of it.

The figures

The first one is being published now.

  • What people look for when they look for a Mac music visualiser — of 660 people who visited between 19 October and 12 November 2026, 215 reached a shared vibescape and 101 reached a comparison page.

How they are counted

Every number here is a count of a group of people, never a record of a person. No published figure covers fewer than fifty people, and any group smaller than that is merged into a bigger one or left out. Nothing identifying a visitor goes into a figure, and a figure cannot be worked backwards into an individual.

Measurement can be switched off at any time from the link in the footer of every page, and any measurement you switch off stops counting towards these figures. If you would rather we did not publish figures that include your visit at all, say so and we will take your visits out of the count.
